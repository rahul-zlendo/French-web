What to say

“Let’s evaluate one of your plans, review the 3D presentation outputs and scope how this fits your current tools. We will confirm the annual licence, rendering charges and evaluation terms before you decide.”

Proof to prepare

One authorised sample plan; approved input/output formats; a real conversion recording; output quality and failure cases; measured preparation time; a written API price schedule.

Discovery questions

  • Do you need a standalone design tool or an API inside your application?
  • How many people, projects, renders and 360° views are required?
  • Which output formats and corrections matter in client presentations?
  • What does your present workflow cost in licences and preparation time?

Do not say: “cheaper than competitors”, “unlimited rendering”, “faster conversion”, “guaranteed savings” or “all features available through API” without evidence.
